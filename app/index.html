Area outlook

A season-ahead outlook for 2 km areas, made for lenders. We set it aside to focus on farmers; it had a pass mark written before the code, so its result is reported here.

How to read it: each hexagon is a 2 km patch of farmland (a dot until you zoom in), coloured by the chance that every dam in it fell to ~0% full (no water seen at a clear look) between October and March. Two maps: the rainfall-only score uses rain alone; the DamDays outlook uses each dam's own satellite history.

What it can't do: it ranks areas, not drought years. It is good at saying which areas are more exposed in a season; whether a whole season will be dry is a different job, best left to the Bureau of Meteorology's climate outlooks.
