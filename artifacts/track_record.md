# Track record: how often the days-left promise held, dam by dam (2016-2026 backtest)

Generated by `scripts/18_track_record.py` on 2026-10-03 14:28. Data: `app/data/real/track_record.json` (format: `app/DATA_CONTRACT.md`).

> **What this is.** For each dam the app shows: how often our cautious days-left promise ("at least N days before it drops below a third") held on that dam, over the forecasts the frozen model made for July 2016 to June 2026, years it never trained on (it learned only from data before July 2016). One forecast per clear satellite look while the dam was above a third and had refilled.

> **Not a new test.** These are the same ten test years that were scored once on Fri 2 Oct 2026 (artifacts/test_results.md), counted dam by dam; nothing new was scored. Those years were looked at before the event, so they may flatter the model slightly. The forecasts a farmer gets today come from the same recipe refitted on all data to September 2026, not from this frozen model.

- **941 dams** in the app (894 on the NSW Central West map, 47 on the demo farms in western Victoria / SE South Australia). **929** have at least 5 judged past forecasts; 12 show "not enough history".

- **Spread:** 520 dams held 9 times in 10 or more (62 every time); 409 held less often than 9 in 10, 101 less than 8 in 10, 21 less than 7 in 10 and 1 less than half the time. Lowest 200 in 1,000, highest 1,000.
- **All these dams together:** held 167,155 of 183,908 judged forecasts (909 in 1,000); a typical dam has 187 judged forecasts over 10 seasons.
- **"Likely" calls** (a chance of 5 in 10 or more): 10,380 of 17,710 were followed by a fall below a third within 90 days.

## How it is counted

- Promise: "at least N days above a third", N as the app and the text show it (whole days, rounded down; "6 months+" is judged at 180 days). It held if the dam stayed above a third for at least N days.
- A forecast is judged only if the satellite archive watched the dam for at least N days after it (to 2026-08-15: the archive ends 2026-09-14, less 30 days to confirm a fall), whatever happened. Forecasts with too few clear looks to know the answer are left out (as in the test).
- A dam with fewer than 5 judged forecasts shows "not enough history".
- Likely calls: forecasts that gave a chance of 5 in 10 or more of falling below a third within 90 days (rounded as the text rounds it), with a known answer; fell = it fell below a third within those 90 days.
- Seasons (by_season): the July-June years with at least one judged forecast, each with [held, judged]; "2016" is July 2016 to June 2017.
- Farm roll-up: the farm's dams with a record, added up (forecasts on nearby dams often share one dry spell, so this is not many independent checks).
